# Institutional marks

Affiliation logos for the authors section, shown at full colour and a
uniform display height.

Files `NN-slug.webp` are ordered by the author superscript number in
`index.html`. Each is trimmed to its visible bounding box and exported at
72 px height (WebP, quality 92), then constrained in CSS.

## Sources

Most marks are the institution's own wordmark, resolved from its Wikidata
"logo image" (P154) claim on Wikimedia Commons (public-domain text logos).

Two were replaced with the file published by the institution itself:

- `04-uga.webp` — brand.uga.edu, horizontal university logo
- `16-tamu.webp` — tamu.edu, primary Texas A&M University lockup

The earlier Texas A&M file was the athletics "aTm" monogram, and the
earlier UCL file was an unofficial coat of arms; neither belonged here.

## Typeset fallbacks

Five affiliations have no freely licensed official mark, so their names
are set in type instead of using an invented or unrelated logo:

- University of Toronto
- Tundra Solutions
- University of Wisconsin–Madison (only a seal is freely available)
- University College London (only an unofficial coat of arms)
- MatrAIx

Replace a text cell with an image only if the institution supplies its
own file.

These are the institutions' own marks and remain their trademarks; they
are used here to identify an author's affiliation.
